Common questions

How much does it cost to fix my marketing?

Every project gets a fixed price. After a free diagnostic call, you receive a written quote that lists what's broken and what it takes to fix, before any work starts.

Do you offer monthly retainers?

No. Boise Marketing Mechanic works project by project. When the repair is done, you get your marketing back along with a write-up and walkthrough so you can keep it running yourself.

Why did my business drop in Google search results?

Common causes include Google algorithm updates, AI Overviews answering searches before people click, Google Business Profile problems, technical site issues, and competitors improving their pages. A diagnostic narrows down which one applies to you.

Why does my Google Ads budget run out without bringing in leads?

Usually it's broad keyword matching, missing negative keywords, poor location targeting, or broken conversion tracking that leaves Google optimizing for the wrong thing. These are fixable, often within a single project.

Does my small business need to keep up with AI marketing?

Not with every trend. Most small businesses do fine with a solid website, an accurate Google Business Profile, and ads and emails that work. AI is worth adopting where it clearly saves you time.
